MCMF records new memecoin launches as they appear, grades each one at first sight, and flags common risks. We publish a small number of alerts, show our results openly (including the misses), and are upfront about what the data can and cannot tell you.

How it works

New launches are captured from public on-chain and market sources into a time-stamped research log. Each is graded at first sight on early market activity and given risk flags for common warning signs. Higher-grade launches are sent as alerts. Every launch remains available as data.

Grades are a research tool, not a prediction. Most new tokens fail. Missing data is shown as missing rather than guessed. Results are published openly — including the misses.

Data sources & assessment

Public on-chain data

Launch detection, bonding-curve or liquidity progress, deployer activity, and basic token metadata across supported chains (Solana, Base, Ethereum and others where public data is available).

Market activity

Early volume, liquidity depth, and bonding-curve completion percentage where the protocol exposes it. Used only for first-sight grading.

Risk flags

Holder concentration, unusual deployer patterns, and other observable warning signs when the data exists. Flags are explicit; absence of a flag is not a safety claim.

Assessment process

Point-in-time capture → simple A/B/C/D grade on early metrics → risk flags attached → higher grades eligible for alerts. No predictive model, no guarantees. Full results (hits and misses) remain visible.
